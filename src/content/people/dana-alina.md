Dana Alina leads the interstellar medium group at Nazarbayev University and
supervises its students. Her PhD at IRAP, Toulouse (2015) analysed polarized
dust emission in Planck data. She works on interstellar magnetic fields with
Planck, PILOT, JCMT and ALMA polarization data, and with the motions of atomic
gas in the Large Magellanic Cloud.
