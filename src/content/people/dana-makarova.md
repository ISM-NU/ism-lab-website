Studies how interstellar gas gathers into filaments and hub-filament systems, where massive stars and clusters form. Combines magnetohydrodynamic simulations with synthetic molecular-line observations to test how much of their 3D structure and gas inflow an observer can recover, and uses dust polarization to study how turbulence and the magnetic field shape filaments.
