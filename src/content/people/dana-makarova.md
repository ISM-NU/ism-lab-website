Studies how interstellar gas gathers into filaments and hub-filament systems, where massive stars and clusters form. Uses magnetohydrodynamic simulations and synthetic molecular-line observations to test how much of a hub's 3D structure and gas inflow an observer can recover, and dust polarization to study how turbulence and the magnetic field shape filaments.
