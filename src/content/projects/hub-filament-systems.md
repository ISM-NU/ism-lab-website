A hub-filament system is a dense clump fed by several filaments that converge
on it. Massive stars and clusters often form in such hubs, so the gas flow
along the filaments may set how fast a future massive star grows. In
position-position-velocity data, projection and line-of-sight confusion hide
the true 3D structure. We use a three-dimensional magnetohydrodynamic
simulation with synthetic molecular-line observations to test how much of that
structure and of the inflow an observer can recover.
