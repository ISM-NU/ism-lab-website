---
title: "Optical polarimetry"
code: "OPT"
summary: "We develop an optical polarimetric facility at the 1 m telescope of the Assy-Turgen Observatory."
order: 5
instruments: ["Assy-Turgen 1 m telescope"]
---

The Assy-Turgen Observatory stands at 2750 m, 85 km east of Almaty. We
develop a polarimeter for its 1 m telescope and install the software
that runs it. Starlight passing through dusty clouds becomes polarized along
the magnetic field, so the instrument will measure the field in front of
individual stars, a complement to the dust emission maps from Planck.
